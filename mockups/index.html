What this does not claim

A rule that never touches the graph beats the graph score here. “Every transaction from an account under 30 days old” separates the held-out split perfectly. Ring accounts in this benchmark are uniformly young, so account age is doing work the graph gets credit for. The Benchmark tab reports it next to our own number rather than around it.

Removing the structural signals costs nothing. Leave one group out at a time and device, IP and instrument are all free to drop. Only the behavioural and refund group is load-bearing.

No model is wired behind the explanation. Narration is composed from the scorer's own evidence strings, and the console says so on screen. The detector never depended on it.
